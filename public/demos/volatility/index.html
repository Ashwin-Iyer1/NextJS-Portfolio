03 / THE MODEL BOUNDARY

IV and historical volatility
answer different questions.

Implied volatility is the Black–Scholes parameter that matches the entered premium. Historical volatility is the sample standard deviation of daily log returns, annualized using 252 trading days. A gap does not guarantee convergence or a profitable trade.

This European model assumes constant volatility and continuous dividends. American exercise, discrete dividends, spreads, assignment timing, changing volatility and taxes are outside it. The covered-call diagram is a terminal payoff, not a backtest.

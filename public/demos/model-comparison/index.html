A comparison is only as good
as its experiment design.

The included lanes occupy the original GPT and Gemini slots, but contain hand-authored selections. The invented tickers and deterministic price paths are a software demonstration, not actual model outputs or evidence that either model outperforms.

For a real study, freeze prompts, model versions, news cutoffs, responses and selections before the execution timestamp. Use a documented adjusted-price source, retain rejected runs, and evaluate many independent periods. A favorable replay alone does not validate a strategy.

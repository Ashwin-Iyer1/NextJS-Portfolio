A probability gap
is not an arbitrage test.

Buying two similarly priced outcomes does not establish a guaranteed payoff. This lab restricts the calculation to matched quantities of complementary YES and NO tokens for one binary condition, then accounts for the actual ask levels needed.

Even a positive modeled difference is conditional on executable liquidity and correct settlement mechanics. It is not a live opportunity or an order instruction.
